# v0.22.4

## Security

Workflow admission now refuses `spec.templateDefaults.dag` and
`spec.templateDefaults.steps`. Argo could add their inline containers after
Oberth applied source and credential protections, allowing those containers to
mount server-owned volumes without the protections. Declare tasks and steps in
concrete templates instead. Container, script, and metadata defaults remain
subject to their existing admission and protection rules.

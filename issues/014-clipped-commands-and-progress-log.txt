ISSUE 014: Narrow terminal clips setup commands and hides useful progress detail

Status: Open
Reported: 2026-10-04
Affected version: Oberth v0.17.9 (6f40126bc8f6), macOS

Priority: Medium
Evidence: Observed in the live 80-column terminal; not inferred from the wide screenshots.

Reproduction and observed behavior
Run interactive setup in an 80-column terminal. Inspect long public-key paths,
fingerprint retrieval commands, the credential ceremony and completion page.
Commands and explanatory text extend beyond the visible width; progress mainly
shows the latest log line, making earlier warnings difficult to inspect.

Expected behavior
Wrap long text and commands, support scrolling when content exceeds terminal
height, and provide an accessible log/details view. Provide copyable complete
commands or save them intact in the non-secret report.

Acceptance criteria
At 80 columns every required action is readable or accessible through an
explicit scrolling/copy/details control. Preserve secret masking in any
persistent diagnostic log. Previous failures remain available for inspection.
